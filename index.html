What the case study proves

I didn't need to love roofing.
I needed to understand what was at stake for the person buying it.

A roof is not content to the homeowner paying for it. It's their home, their money, their stress, their safety and a decision they do not want to get wrong. Once I understood the problem from their side, I could build marketing around the questions, proof and reassurance that actually mattered.

Research before messaging

I look for the questions people are already asking before I decide what the marketing should answer.

For home care, that means getting specific about the fears behind the search instead of writing generic copy about compassion and trust.
